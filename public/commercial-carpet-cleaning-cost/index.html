COMMERCIAL CARPET CLEANING COST

How much does commercial carpet cleaning cost?

Commercial carpet cleaning cost depends on area, method, access, furniture, soil level and labour. Build a commercial quote from actual site costs.

What changes the cost?

Large areas can still require significant setup, furniture management and access time. A low unit rate becomes unprofitable quickly if productivity assumptions are too optimistic.

  • measured carpet area
  • encapsulation, extraction or other method
  • furniture density
  • site access and working hours
  • crew productivity
  • chemicals and machine wear
  • travel, overhead and margin

Build the site price before the area rate

Calculate crew-hours and direct treatment costs first. Then divide the finished selling price by area to create a site-specific unit rate for the proposal or tender.

Is commercial carpet cleaning priced per square foot or square metre?

Often, but the rate should be derived from the site scope, method and productivity rather than copied from another job.
