DEEP CLEANING COST

How much should a deep clean cost?

Deep cleaning cost should reflect extra labour, detailed tasks, supplies, travel and overhead. Use a practical pricing method instead of guesswork.

Why deep cleans cost more

A deep clean is not just a standard clean with a percentage added. It often changes the task list, pace of work and consumable use.

  • extra person-hours
  • inside cupboards or appliances where included
  • scale, grease and heavy-soil removal
  • additional cloths and chemicals
  • equipment and laundry
  • travel, overhead and margin

Quote the deeper scope

Write the scope first, estimate the paid time for each cleaner, add direct costs and then apply the same overhead and margin model used elsewhere in the business.

Should a deep clean have a fixed price?

It can, but the fixed price should be based on a realistic estimate of person-hours and direct costs.
