FULL CAR DETAILING COST

How much should a full car detail cost?

Full car detailing cost depends on interior and exterior labour, vehicle condition, products, correction work and overhead. Use a complete costing method.

Define the package before pricing it

A full detail can mean very different things from one business to another. Maintenance detailing, deep interior cleaning and paint-correction work should not be treated as the same service with minor price adjustments.

  • vehicle type and size
  • interior condition
  • wash and decontamination stages
  • paint enhancement or correction where included
  • product and towel usage
  • total paid labour time
  • premises or mobile overhead

Cost each labour stage

Break the package into labour stages, add the products and equipment costs created by each stage, then apply overhead and margin.

Why do full detailing prices vary?
