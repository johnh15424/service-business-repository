HOUSE CLEANING COST

How much should house cleaning cost?

House cleaning cost depends on labour time, property size, frequency, service scope, supplies, travel and overhead. Build a repeatable quote.

What changes the cost?

Property size matters, but cleaning time is the real economic driver. Two homes with the same floor area can take very different amounts of labour depending on condition, layout, pets, clutter and the scope agreed with the customer.

  • property size and layout
  • number of cleaners and paid hours
  • regular clean versus first-time clean
  • bathrooms, kitchens and high-effort areas
  • cleaning supplies and laundry
  • travel, admin and business overhead

Price from person-hours

Estimate person-hours for the visit, add consumables and travel, allocate a realistic share of monthly overhead and then protect the required margin.
