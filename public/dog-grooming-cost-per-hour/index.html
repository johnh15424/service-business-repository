DOG GROOMING HOURLY COST

What should a dog groomer charge per hour?

Work out a sustainable dog grooming hourly cost by including labour, overhead, consumables, equipment wear and target margin.

What belongs in the hourly cost?

An hourly rate only works if the hourly cost base is complete. Looking at wage alone understates what the business must recover. A sustainable rate has to fund both the person doing the groom and the overhead that exists even when the table is empty.

  • owner or employee labour cost
  • employer on-costs where relevant
  • shampoo and conditioner
  • blade sharpening and clipper wear
  • utilities and laundry
  • booking software, insurance and rent
  • unpaid setup and clean-up time

Build the rate from the real cost base

Start with the loaded labour cost per paid hour, add a fair hourly share of overhead and equipment costs, then convert the required margin into a selling price. Use the calculator for a real appointment and compare the result with the hourly rate you currently rely on.

Is hourly pricing suitable for dog grooming?

It can be useful internally, but customers are often quoted by service. The hourly figure is best used as a profitability check behind the service price.

What costs should be included in a grooming hourly rate?

Labour, payroll on-costs, consumables, utilities, equipment wear, software, insurance, rent or premises costs and other recurring overhead.

What is the difference between markup and margin?

Markup adds a percentage to cost. Margin is the percentage of the selling price retained after costs, so the two are not interchangeable.
