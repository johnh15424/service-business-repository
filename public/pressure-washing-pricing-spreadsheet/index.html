FOR PRESSURE WASHING BUSINESS OWNERS

Pressure Washing pricing spreadsheet for repeatable job costing

Use a structured pricing model to turn labour, direct costs, travel, overhead, fees and margin into a repeatable price instead of relying on guesswork.

A spreadsheet should price the work, not just the area

Square metres or square feet are useful for checking a result, but they should not be the whole pricing model. Access, soiling, treatment requirements, setup, drainage and surface sensitivity can all change labour time and consumable usage.

Include the costs that are easy to miss

A robust pressure-washing workbook should recover crew-hours, detergents and treatments, water or generator cost, equipment wear, vehicle cost, paid travel, overhead and transaction fees. It should also protect a minimum call-out where a small job still consumes travel and setup time.

Turn estimates into a repeatable quote process

Once the job economics are solved, the result can be expressed as a per-area rate for checking consistency. This keeps the quote commercially grounded while still giving the business a simple benchmark for future work.

Try the pricing method before buying

The free calculator lets you model one job in the browser. If the method fits your business, the Pro Toolkit gives you the reusable downloadable workbook for ongoing pricing and planning.
