Build handyman prices from labour, materials, collection time, tool wear, travel, disposal, overhead and target margin.

Separate labour from the rest of the job

A handyman quote often includes materials, fixings, collection time, travel, disposal and tool wear as well as hands-on labour. A reusable pricing spreadsheet makes those components explicit before a selling price is chosen.

Use minimum charges for short call-outs

A short repair can still require travel, parking, customer communication and setup. Pricing only the minutes spent with tools in hand can understate the real cost of the appointment.

Review estimated versus actual time

When jobs repeatedly run longer than expected, improve the estimating assumptions rather than simply absorbing the overrun. A pricing system should make that feedback loop easier.
