Mobile Car Detailing pricing spreadsheet for repeatable job costing

Use a structured pricing model to turn labour, direct costs, travel, overhead, fees and margin into a repeatable price instead of relying on guesswork.

Build detailing prices from the job underneath

A detailing pricing spreadsheet should separate the actual cost drivers of the appointment: paid labour, vehicle condition, chemicals, water, electricity, equipment wear, travel, overhead and payment fees. A single market-average price cannot account for all of those variables.

Use service packages without losing the economics

Maintenance details, interior details, full details and paint-enhancement work can all have different labour and consumable profiles. A reusable workbook lets the business keep package names customer-friendly while maintaining a cost model underneath each package.

What to review after each job

Compare estimated time with actual time, note chemical usage and check whether travel or setup was understated. Repeated overruns are a pricing signal. The aim is to improve the assumptions rather than simply expect the operator to work faster.

The free calculator lets you model one job in the browser. If the method fits your business, the Pro Toolkit gives you the reusable downloadable workbook for ongoing pricing and planning.
