FOR PET SITTING BUSINESSES

Pet Sitting Cost: How to Price Visits and Stays

Start with the complete service commitment: travel, entry, care tasks, updates, key handling and any special instructions. Then allocate overhead and apply the target margin. The same framework works for short visits and longer stays, but capacity becomes more important as bookings get longer.

What drives pet sitting cost?

Visit or stay duration

Count feeding, cleaning, medication notes, owner updates and other agreed care tasks, not just time spent playing with the pet.

Travel and key handling

Key collection, access arrangements and repeated journeys can create meaningful unpaid time unless they are represented in the price.

Number and needs of pets

Multiple animals, medication and special-care instructions can change both time and responsibility.

Overnight capacity

An overnight booking can restrict where the sitter works and how many other visits fit around it. That capacity cost should be understood before setting the rate.

Insurance and consumables

Insurance, cleaning supplies, waste bags and small equipment belong in the business cost base.

A practical pet sitting pricing model

  1. Define the visit or stay and the exact care tasks included.
  2. Estimate paid service time and travel.
  3. Add key handling, consumables and other direct costs.
  4. Allocate overhead across realistic monthly bookings.
  5. Include payment fees and predictable leakage.
  6. Apply target retained margin.
  7. Check longer stays against the capacity they remove from the diary.
  8. Add tax separately where required.

Common mistakes

  • Pricing a visit only from time inside the home.
  • Ignoring key collection and customer communication.
  • Using the same rate for routine and medication visits.
  • Treating overnight stays as simple hourly labour without considering capacity.
  • Setting holiday premiums without first knowing the normal cost base.

Review the real time commitment

Track actual door-to-door time, care tasks and customer communication. If a supposedly short visit regularly expands because of access, medication or multiple pets, update the service assumptions instead of letting the difference erode margin.
