FOR DOG GROOMING BUSINESS OWNERS

Dog Grooming pricing spreadsheet for repeatable job costing

Use a structured pricing model to turn labour, direct costs, travel, overhead, fees and margin into a repeatable price instead of relying on guesswork.

Price grooming appointments from capacity and cost

A grooming pricing spreadsheet should connect appointment time to the economics of the business. Breed, coat condition, size and service scope all affect labour time, while shampoo, blades, laundry, utilities and equipment wear create additional costs.

Protect the value of scarce appointment time

Salon capacity is limited by available groomer-hours and stations. Underpricing a long appointment can displace more profitable work even when the booking itself appears busy. A repeatable costing model helps identify that difference.

Review services rather than copying a generic price list

Use market prices as a sense-check, not as the pricing engine. Build the required price from the business's own labour, operating cost, overhead and target margin, then compare the result with the local market and service positioning.

Try the pricing method before buying

The free calculator lets you model one job in the browser. If the method fits your business, the Pro Toolkit gives you the reusable downloadable workbook for ongoing pricing and planning.
