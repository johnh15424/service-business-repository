How much should mobile car detailing cost?

Mobile car detailing cost depends on service time, vehicle condition, products, travel, water, power and overhead. Build a profitable mobile quote.

What changes the cost?

Mobile detailing adds travel and setup costs that fixed-site businesses may not carry in the same way. If driving, loading, water or power arrangements are not recovered, a job can lose margin quickly.

  • vehicle size and condition
  • interior and exterior service scope
  • labour time
  • chemicals, towels and consumables
  • mobile travel and vehicle costs
  • water and power setup
  • equipment wear and business overhead
