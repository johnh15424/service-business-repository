Lawn care pricing spreadsheet for repeatable job costing

Build lawn care prices from crew time, fuel, machinery wear, disposal, travel, overhead and target margin instead of relying on a flat mowing rate.

Price the route and the job

A lawn care pricing spreadsheet should account for more than mowing time. Travel between jobs, setup, fuel, machinery wear, disposal, crew cost and seasonal capacity all affect the economics of a route.

Use a minimum charge deliberately

Small jobs still consume travel, loading and unloading time. A minimum charge can protect the business from completing work that produces too little contribution for the time slot.

Review revenue per crew-hour

Once a job price is built from cost and margin, compare it with the total crew-hours consumed. That gives a useful capacity check when deciding which services and routes deserve more space in the schedule.
