COMMERCIAL WINDOW CLEANING COST

How much does commercial window cleaning cost?

Commercial window cleaning cost depends on glass area, access, height, frequency, labour and site requirements. Build a consistent commercial quote.

Commercial work includes non-cleaning time

Site access, sign-in, moving between elevations and working around staff or customers all consume paid labour and should be part of the quote.

  • glass area and pane layout
  • access equipment or water-fed pole reach
  • site induction and working-hour restrictions
  • crew size and labour time
  • frequency of service
  • travel, water and equipment
  • insurance and overhead

Derive the unit rate from the site

Cost the full site visit first, then convert the finished price into a per-pane or per-area benchmark if that helps tendering.

Does cleaning frequency affect price?

Yes. More frequent work can reduce soil level and improve route efficiency, but the recurring quote should still cover the real visit cost.
