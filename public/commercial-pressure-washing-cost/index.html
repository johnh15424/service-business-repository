COMMERCIAL PRESSURE WASHING COST

How much does commercial pressure washing cost?

Commercial pressure washing cost depends on area, surface, access, setup, treatment, labour, water and travel. Build a quote from the real job costs.

What changes the cost?

Commercial work is more variable than a simple residential wash because mobilisation, access, working hours, water supply and site constraints can materially change the job. A square-foot or square-metre rate is only useful after those costs are understood.

  • site area and surface type
  • contamination level and treatment method
  • setup, access and safety requirements
  • crew size and paid labour hours
  • water, fuel and chemical usage
  • travel, equipment wear and overhead

Build the price before the unit rate

Calculate crew-hours and direct costs first. Then divide the finished price by the measured area if a unit rate is useful for quoting or benchmarking. This prevents a headline unit rate from hiding an unprofitable setup.

Is commercial pressure washing priced by square foot?

Often, but the unit rate should come after the job costs are understood. Access, setup, treatment and minimum charges can make the same area cost very different amounts.

What increases commercial pressure washing cost?

Difficult access, heavy contamination, specialist chemicals, water constraints, night work, traffic management, long travel and additional safety controls.
