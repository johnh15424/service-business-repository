Window Cleaning pricing spreadsheet for repeatable job costing

Build window cleaning prices from labour, access, purified water, equipment wear, travel, overhead, minimum charge and target margin.

Window count is only one pricing input

Pane count can help standardise quoting, but access, height, first-clean condition, screens, frames and travel can materially change the time and cost required.

Recurring rounds need route economics

Repeat work may be faster to complete, but recurring jobs also need to justify the travel and schedule slot they consume. Review revenue per stop and per working hour as well as the customer-facing price.

Protect small jobs with a minimum

A minimum charge can recover the trip, setup and administration that exist even when the actual glass-cleaning time is short.
