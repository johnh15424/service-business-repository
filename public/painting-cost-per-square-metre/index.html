PAINTING COST PER SQUARE METRE

How do you calculate painting cost per square metre?

Calculate painting cost per square metre from preparation, coats, labour, materials, access and overhead instead of using a generic rate.

Area alone is not enough

A square-metre rate is useful only when the surface and scope are comparable. Preparation can take more time than rolling paint, so an area-only rate can badly understate the job.

  • measured paintable area
  • surface condition and preparation
  • number of coats
  • cutting-in and detail work
  • paint and sundries
  • access equipment
  • labour productivity
  • overhead and margin

Calculate the job first

Estimate total labour and materials, calculate the complete selling price and then divide by measured area. Keep separate adjustments for materially different preparation and access conditions.

Should painting always be priced per square metre?

No. It is a useful benchmark, but preparation, trim, access and room complexity can make area-only pricing misleading.
