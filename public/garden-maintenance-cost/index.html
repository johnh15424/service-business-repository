GARDEN MAINTENANCE COST

How much should garden maintenance cost?

Garden maintenance cost depends on labour, machinery, waste, travel and task mix. Price the full visit instead of relying on one hourly guess.

What changes the cost?

Garden maintenance visits often combine several tasks with different productivity and equipment requirements.

  • mowing, edging and trimming
  • hedge or shrub work
  • leaf and debris clearance
  • crew-hours
  • machinery fuel and wear
  • green waste disposal
  • travel and overhead

Price the visit, not just the hour

Build the visit from total paid crew-hours and direct job costs. For recurring work, review actual completion times periodically so the quote stays aligned with the real workload across seasons.

Should seasonal changes affect pricing?

They can. Growth rate, leaf fall and weather can change the work required, so recurring scopes should be reviewed.
