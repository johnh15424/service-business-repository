HANDYMAN HOURLY RATE

What should a handyman charge per hour?

Build a profitable handyman hourly rate from labour, travel, tools, overhead and margin instead of copying a competitor's rate.

What belongs in the hourly rate?

The selling rate has to fund more than the hour spent at the customer's property. Quoting, travel, collecting materials, maintenance and administration reduce the number of hours that can actually be billed.

  • owner or employee labour cost
  • paid travel and loading time
  • vehicle cost
  • tool replacement and consumables
  • insurance, software and admin
  • unbillable time
  • target margin

Build the rate from real billable capacity

Start with loaded labour cost, recover overhead across realistic billable hours or jobs and protect the margin target. For fixed-price work, use the hourly model internally as a profitability check.

Should a handyman charge by the hour or by the job?

Both models can work. Fixed-price jobs are still safer when built from an internal labour and cost estimate.
