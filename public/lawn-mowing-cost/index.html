LAWN MOWING COST

How much should lawn mowing cost?

Lawn mowing cost depends on crew time, lawn size, access, travel, fuel, equipment wear and disposal. Build a repeatable mowing quote.

What changes the cost?

Lawn area is useful, but time is the economic driver. Gates, slopes, edging, obstacles, wet grass and collection can make two similar-sized lawns require very different amounts of paid time.

  • lawn size and mowing time
  • crew size
  • access and obstacles
  • travel distance
  • fuel and consumables
  • mower and trimmer wear
  • green waste handling
  • business overhead

Price the whole visit

Include loading, travel and finishing work, then add fuel, equipment wear and disposal before applying overhead and margin.

Why use a minimum mowing charge?

Small lawns still require travel, unloading, setup and administration, so a minimum helps recover those fixed visit costs.
