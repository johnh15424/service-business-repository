CARPET CLEANING COST

How much should carpet cleaning cost?

Carpet cleaning cost depends on area, rooms, soil level, labour, chemicals, equipment and travel. Build a repeatable quote from the real job.

What changes the cost?

Room count is convenient for customers, but the real cost comes from labour and treatment. Large rooms, heavy soil, stairs, furniture and specialist spotting can all change the time required.

  • room count or measured area
  • soil level and pre-treatment
  • furniture moving where included
  • setup and extraction time
  • chemicals and spot treatments
  • machine wear and utilities
  • travel and overhead

Cost the complete cleaning process

Estimate setup, treatment, cleaning and pack-down time, then add direct chemical and machine costs. Apply overhead and margin to the full job rather than pricing only the extraction minutes.
