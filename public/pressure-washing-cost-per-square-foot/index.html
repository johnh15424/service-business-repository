PRESSURE WASHING COST PER SQUARE FOOT

How do you calculate pressure washing cost per square foot?

Calculate pressure washing cost per square foot from labour, chemicals, travel, overhead and margin instead of copying a generic market rate.

A unit rate should come after the job cost

Two jobs with the same area can require very different labour and chemical inputs. Surface type, contamination, access and minimum charges all change the economics.

  • total measured area
  • crew-hours and labour cost
  • surface cleaner or lance productivity
  • chemical and treatment usage
  • travel and vehicle cost
  • equipment wear and overhead
  • minimum charge and margin target

Simple method

Calculate the complete selling price first, then divide by the measured square footage. Keep a minimum charge for smaller work and avoid forcing specialist surfaces into the same unit rate.

Should one square-foot rate be used for every surface?

No. Concrete, siding, roofs and specialist treatments can have different productivity, chemical and risk profiles.

Can the same method be used for square metres?

Yes. The costing method is identical; only the area unit changes.
