WINDOW CLEANING COST

How much should window cleaning cost?

Window cleaning cost depends on pane count, access, method, labour, travel and overhead. Build a quote from the actual job rather than guesswork.

What changes the cost?

Counting windows is useful, but access and method often determine labour time. French panes, conservatories, awkward elevations and interior access can all change the effort required even when the window count looks similar.

  • window count and size
  • inside, outside or both
  • ground-level versus high access
  • water-fed pole or traditional method
  • crew time
  • travel and pure-water costs
  • equipment wear and overhead

Price the labour first

Estimate complete person-hours, add water, consumables and travel, allocate overhead and protect the target margin. A per-window figure can then be used as a check.

Should window cleaning be priced per window?

It can be a useful quoting unit, but access, pane type, height and method should still be reflected in the price.
