HANDYMAN JOB COST

How much should a handyman charge per job?

Handyman job cost depends on labour, materials, travel, tools and overhead. Build fixed-price jobs from a consistent costing method.

What changes the job cost?

A fixed price gives the customer certainty, but it should not be a guess. The business still needs an internal breakdown of time, materials and overhead.

  • estimated labour hours
  • materials and collection time
  • consumables and fixings
  • travel and parking
  • specialist tools or hire
  • risk and scope uncertainty
  • overhead and margin

Turn scope into a fixed price

Define the scope, estimate paid hours including material collection where relevant, add direct costs and apply the same margin model used across the business.
