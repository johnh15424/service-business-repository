INTERIOR PAINTING COST

How much should interior painting cost?

Interior painting cost depends on preparation, rooms, surface condition, coats, trim, materials and labour. Build a complete decorating quote.

Preparation is part of the price

Filling, sanding, masking, moving furniture, cutting in and clean-up all consume paid time before and after the visible painting stage.

  • wall and ceiling area
  • surface repairs and preparation
  • number of coats
  • doors, skirting and trim
  • masking and protection
  • paint and sundries
  • labour time and access
  • overhead and margin

Break the job into labour stages

Separate preparation, painting and finishing time, add the paint and consumables required, then allocate overhead before applying the target margin.

What makes interior painting more expensive?

Poor surface condition, detailed trim, high ceilings, multiple colours, furniture handling and extra coats can all add time.
